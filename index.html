02 / How I build

Evidence before confidence.

I build systems that make decisions traceable, capture what happened, and improve with every verified outcome.

  1. 01

    Frame the decision

    Define the objective, constraints, and success criteria.

  2. 02

    Run the system

    Execute with tools, models, and data. Log the context.

  3. 03

    Capture the evidence

    Collect artifacts, results, measurements, and provenance.

  4. 04

    Learn from the outcome

    Evaluate what worked, record why, and update the system.
